JSPS Research Fellow (DC1)

Kurosawa Laboratory (X-Lab), Department of Energy Engineering,
Graduate School of Engineering, Nagoya University

My research focuses on the crystal growth of inorganic luminescent materials, particularly halide scintillators, and on the characterization of their optical and radiation-detection properties. By combining these experiments with electronic-structure analysis based on first-principles calculations, I aim to elucidate the underlying luminescence mechanisms and to apply these materials to neutron and gamma-ray detection.
